## Step 2 — Add to Your Home Screen (Most Important)
Adding the app to your home screen provides:
- Full-screen native experience with no browser URL bars
- Permanent device storage protection from Safari auto-clearing
- 100% instant offline loading

### 📱 iPhone / iPad (Safari)
1. Open your link in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share button** (square with upward arrow).
3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
4. Tap **Add** — the icon appears on your home screen!

### 🤖 Android (Chrome)
1. Open your link in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the **three-dot menu** (top right).
3. Tap **"Add to Home Screen"** or **"Install App"**.

### 💻 Mac / Windows (Chrome or Edge)
1. Open in Chrome or Edge.
2. Click the install icon in the URL bar (computer with ↓ arrow).
3. Click **Install**.

## Your 4 Neurodivergent Superpowers

### 1. 🧮 Safe to Spend Today
One clear number inside your central dial. Check it before making discretionary purchases. If it’s green, spend guilt-free!

### 2. ⏳ 48-Hour Impulse Vault
Impulse spending urge? Park it in the vault. If you still want it in 48 hours, buy it with zero guilt. 82% of impulses fade on their own.

### 3. 🧟 Zombie Mode
Overwhelmed or burned out? Tap **Zombie Mode** in the top bar. Everything disappears except one number: *"Can I afford this right now?"*

### 4. 📝 Brain Dump Scratchpad
Money thoughts cluttering your head? Dump them in the Notes tab. It auto-saves instantly so nothing gets lost.
