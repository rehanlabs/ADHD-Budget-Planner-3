☀️ ADHD Budget Planner — Zen Sanctuary Guide

A warm, low-stimulation personal finance sanctuary engineered specifically for neurodivergent minds.

1. Device Installation & Offline Setup

iOS Safari Open https://rehanlabs.github.io/ADHD-Budget-Planner-3/ in Safari. Tap the Share button (square with arrow), then select "Add to Home Screen". This secures your data against Safari's 7-day auto-clear policy.
Android Chrome Open the link in Chrome. Tap the three dots (⋮) top right and select "Install App" or "Add to Home Screen".
Desktop & Offline You can also unzip ADHD-Budget-Planner-Offline.zip and double-click index.html to run 100% locally with zero internet required.

2. Guided Walkthrough with Leo 🧘

Tap the Leo's Mindful Tour button in the top bar anytime. Leo guides you step-by-step through setting your pay schedule, managing bills, parking impulsive urges, and creating a stress-free financial habit.

3. Core System Architecture

  • Concentric Radial Allowance Ring: Glance at your single daily safe spend amount inside a calm circular gauge. No overwhelming spreadsheets.
  • 48-Hour Cooldown Hold: Park impulse buying urges before purchase. Over 80% of neurodivergent shopping urges resolve naturally within 48 hours.
  • Fog Mode: An ultra-low stimulation warm amber sanctuary mode for high-stress, sensory-overload days. Strips away everything except what is essential right now.
  • Living Costs & Payday Runway: Automatically reserves mandatory living expenses before calculating your discretionary daily spend.
  • Debt Freedom Calculator: Visual snowball and avalanche payoff simulators that turn anxiety into forward momentum.
  • Mind Dump Scratchpad: A zero-friction mental parking lot to capture financial worries, todo lists, and budget reminders.

4. Monthly Data Backups

Your financial data never touches third-party cloud servers; it remains 100% private in your device's secure browser storage. Once a month, visit Sanctuary Config → Download Backup (.json) to archive your data to iCloud or Google Drive.
